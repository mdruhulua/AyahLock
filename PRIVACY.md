# AyahLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

AyahLock does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.

**What stays on your iPhone.** Your saved ayahs and duas, your settings, and any audio files you import are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widget. Nothing is uploaded.

**Network use.** When you add a Quran passage, the app downloads its text, translations and recitation audio from AlQuran Cloud (api.alquran.cloud and cdn.islamic.network). These requests contain only the surah and ayah numbers and the reciter you chose. Like any website, those servers may see your device's IP address; AlQuran Cloud's own terms and policies apply to them.

**No tracking.** The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

**Deleting your data.** Deleting an item in the app removes it and its audio. Deleting the app removes everything.
